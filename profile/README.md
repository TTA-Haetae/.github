## Telecommunications Technology Association (TTA)

TTA is Korea's ICT standardization and testing & certification organization.
The TTA AI Infrastructure Verification Team builds and operates AI computing
and HPC infrastructure, including GPU clusters, parallel storage systems, and
cloud services, which it provides to Korean industry and research institutions.

This organization documents the system configurations, tuning parameters, and
execution environments of the storage infrastructure we operate, in support of
community reproducibility initiatives.
